## 📊 Project Overview

The **Café Business Performance Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence dashboard developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The purpose of this project is to transform café sales data into meaningful and actionable business insights. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, gross profit, profitability, product performance, costs, payment methods, and monthly trends.

The dashboard is designed to help business stakeholders quickly understand performance and identify areas for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall café revenue and profitability
- Track revenue growth and profit margin
- Monitor Average Order Value (AOV)
- Analyze item-wise sales and gross profit
- Understand labour and food costs
- Analyze revenue by payment method
- Identify monthly profit trends
- Compare product performance
- Present business insights through an interactive dashboard

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Revenue & Profit Analysis

The dashboard provides a high-level overview of:

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Profit Margin
- Revenue Growth
- Average Order Value
- AOV Growth

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of the café's overall financial performance.

---

### 2. Item-wise Gross Profit Analysis

The dashboard analyzes gross profit generated by individual menu items.

The analysis includes products such as:

- Veggie Club Special
- Veggie Paradise
- Veg Overload
- Watermelon Juice

A cumulative profit percentage line is also included to understand how individual products contribute to overall profitability.

---

### 3. Sales Performance by Item

The dashboard compares the sales performance of different café items.

This visualization helps identify:

- High-performing products
- Lower-performing products
- Differences in sales volume
- Product-level performance patterns

---

### 4. Cost Breakdown

The cost breakdown visualization analyzes the major cost components of the café.

The dashboard currently highlights:

- Total Revenue
- Labour Cost
- Food Cost
- Total after costs

This helps provide a better understanding of how operating costs affect overall profitability.

---

### 5. Revenue by Payment Method

Revenue is analyzed across different payment methods:

- UPI
- Online
- Card
- Cash

This provides insights into customer payment preferences and the contribution of each payment channel.

---

### 6. Monthly Gross Profit Analysis

The dashboard includes a month-by-month gross profit analysis for individual menu items.

This helps identify:

- Monthly profit trends
- High-performing months
- Product seasonality
- Changes in item profitability

---

### 7. Interactive Menu Navigation

The dashboard includes an interactive menu/navigation section for different café categories and items, including:

- 🍔 Burger
- 🥗 Club Veg
- 🍟 French Fries
- ☕ Hot Drinks
- 🥤 Juice
- 🍝 Macaroni
- 🍕 Pizza
- 🥪 Sandwich
- 🥬 Veg Mania

This makes the dashboard easier to navigate and explore.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps identify several important business insights:

### 💰 Strong Profitability

The café generates approximately **$39.39K in gross profit** with a **47.62% profit margin**, indicating a strong contribution of revenue toward profitability.

### 📈 Positive Revenue Growth

Revenue growth stands at **9.42%**, showing positive movement compared with the previous period.

### 🧾 Average Order Value

The current Average Order Value is **$25.85**. However, AOV growth is **-0.42%**, suggesting that increasing customer order value could be an opportunity for improvement.

### 🍔 Product-Level Performance

Item-wise analysis helps identify products that contribute more significantly to gross profit and sales performance.

### 💵 Cost Management

The cost breakdown highlights the impact of labour and food costs on overall profitability, providing an opportunity for better cost management.

### 💳 Payment Preferences

Revenue is distributed across UPI, Online, Card, and Cash payment methods, allowing businesses to understand customer payment behaviour.
